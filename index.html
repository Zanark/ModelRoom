WELCOME TO MODEL ROOM

From a thought
to a thing.

  1. Bring an idea. Describe the object, style, dimensions and moving parts. Attach local image references and save a brief.
  2. Make it with your agent. Give the brief and original reference files to your coding agent in this project. The brief stores filenames, not your images.
  3. Look closer. Orbit, zoom, unfold the starter box, change the lighting and explore individual parts.
  4. Take it with you. Download a GLB, then in Blender choose File > Import > glTF 2.0. Meshes and materials remain editable; JavaScript construction logic doesn't become Blender modifiers.

All three shelf entries are color studies of the same original cardboard box. New objects are authored by your agent, not generated from arbitrary text by this page.
